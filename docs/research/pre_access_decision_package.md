writer. RAM sums simultaneous worker buffers plus shared/OS reserve. One
missing finite term means no capacity verdict. The floor is conditional and
belongs to the full simulation, not the four-file Stage-B query.

diagnostic, not a completion bound to multiply by workload. Choose and justify
mean bound, hard envelope or probabilistic service level explicitly. This EC
allows zero paid compute and recommends no new resource ceiling.
