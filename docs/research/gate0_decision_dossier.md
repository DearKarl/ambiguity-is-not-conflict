## Facts

- The current protocol defines conflict only when both modality states are
  determinate; an undefined conflict label is not a compatible label.
- The current data route is restricted coupled MIMIC-CXR/JPG, and no record,
  report, image, or dataset file has been accessed under this dossier.
- Official model documentation names MIMIC/MIMIC-CXR training for BioViL-T.
  BiomedCLIP and SigLIP2 documentation names their broad training sources but
  supplies no patient-level MIMIC exclusion manifest.
- The present construct floors were not calculated to prove four learned
  probes equivalent to chance within `+0.05`.
- The canonical research contract additionally requires the exact model/
  estimator decision, matched implementations, frozen backbone, calibration,
  partitions/sampling, governed breadth choice, and retention boundary at Gate
  0. A later task brief cannot silently defer or override those requirements.
- The TB-0006 binary reductions are exact for learned model outputs:
  self-corrected learned-belief disagreement is
  \((\widehat p_v-\widehat p_t)^2\); a conditional density ratio is a
  classifier logit minus the sampling-prior log-odds; and evidential confident
  disagreement is RCML's occupied projected-distance-times-certainty form.
  External reader frequencies remain measurement variables, not score inputs.

## Inferences

- A clean design should remove known marginal shortcuts by construction and
  use learned probes to discover implementation failure. An underpowered null
  probe result cannot establish artifact absence.
- Unknown pretraining exposure does not make a controlled within-source
  contrast meaningless, but it prevents strict unseen-patient or clean-
  checkpoint wording and weakens generalization evidence.
- None of the three audited pointwise candidates supplies a non-cosmetic new
  estimator. The exact `psi_mag` plug-in estimator remains a population
  specificity estimator for an already frozen score, not a deployable
  pair-level conflict score. Gate 0 must therefore record either a prospective

  a new theory candidate that survives equivalence review. Development may fit
  and tune only within the resulting frozen rules; the fitted instance,
  normalization, link, and complete configuration lock before the one-time
  Month-3 set.
- No target-semantics-preserving `M_t` exists for a truly atomic binary
  assertion. Removing its only state carrier necessarily makes `Y_t`
  undefined; surface corruption is a different control.

## Artifact Decision Detail

### Design invariants required under `G0-ARTIFACT A`

For every patient partition, the generator and validator must enforce:

1. each exact clear image occurs once with its compatible and once with its
   conflicting atomic assertion, so image-only and source provenance are
   label-paired;
2. each exact positive/negative atomic string, template, renderer, punctuation,
   length stratum, and process path has equal condition support across matched
   image-positive and image-negative source blocks;
3. variant generation happens inside the patient/source partition and emits a
   byte-level intervention ledger; and
4. any imbalance, duplicate leakage, hidden condition field, or process-path
   difference is instrument failure before model scoring.

For `MV-1` and `MT-1`, the manipulated modality is intentionally recoverable
as an information-loss arm. Their shortcut audit therefore excludes that
modality and requires exact non-target-modality identity plus matched process/
provenance checks against the within-source reference. A conflict-versus-
compatible probe cannot certify control-arm validity.

These invariants establish only the frozen empirical marginal balance. They do
not establish distributional equality for every unknown feature or prove
artifact absence. The four probes therefore remain useful falsification
instruments. Define orientation-safe recoverability as
`R_j = 0.50 + abs(BA_j - 0.50) = max(BA_j, 1 - BA_j)` on the frozen held-out
pipeline. A lower bound for `R_j` above `0.55` kills the instrument; an interval
crossing `0.55` is inconclusive; an upper bound strictly below `0.55` supports only
that exact probe/population claim. Raw balanced accuracy below `0.50` is not a
pass because flipping its labels recovers the condition.

### Powered equivalence option

If `G0-ARTIFACT B` is selected, the joint claim is the intersection that every
`R_j < 0.55`, against the union null that at least one `R_j >= 0.55`.
Equivalently, each raw `BA_j` must lie strictly inside `(0.45, 0.55)`. An
intersection--union test may test each component equivalence claim at the
stage's nominal alpha without alpha-Bonferroni correction for the conjunctive
global Type-I error; family power remains a separate requirement. Under a
centered Bernoulli-like approximation, a two-sided equivalence acceptance
region, and conservative beta allocation across four probes, illustrative
requirements are about 1,047 Month-3 and 1,757 confirmatory independent
patients before attrition, or about 1,232 and 2,068 screened at 15% loss.
The 1,047-patient figure is only a non-promotable developmental bounded-
recoverability screen: Month 3 cannot establish equivalence or supply
confirmatory artifact evidence. Only a later untouched, fully simulated and
powered confirmation could support the bounded four-probe statement.
Linearly scaling the current reader worksheet gives approximately 1,302
clinical hours through Month 3 and 2,657 hours for the confirmation row alone,
versus the revised proposed 500-hour stage and 1,350-hour cumulative ceilings. This
linear scaling is a planning assumption, not a workload fact. Requiring
two-sided Bonferroni-simultaneous
component intervals raises the corresponding crude figures to about 1,756 and
2,463. Balanced-accuracy structure, trained-probe uncertainty, patient
dependence, the non-smooth orientation, and model selection require simulation
before any figure is approved. Neither option fits the present construct
floors; the older one-sided 1,293/1,976 calculation is not orientation-safe and
cannot be the final design.

## Kill Tree

1. If scope narrowing is rejected and no valid ambiguity-identification route
   exists, stop before implementation.
2. If `MV-1` fails any member of the prospective joint q family
   (`L_bal > 0.10`, `L_present > 0`, `L_absent > 0`), aligned-support/state,
   reliability, evaluable-yield, or reader-sensitivity rule, or `MT-1` fails
   independent validity/reliability, stop and reopen that control; do not search
   alternatives using candidate scores.
3. If a probe demonstrates condition recovery, or a construction invariant
   fails, kill the instrument before interpreting estimator performance.
4. If the frozen pointwise instrument fails `psi_mag`, the `+0.10` material-
   advantage gate, or the downstream `+0.01` gate, kill the Main Track method
   claim. Record a qualified null or deterministic result when warranted.
5. If no strict checkpoint/cohort route exists, narrow the model/generalization
   evidence; if that leaves the central claim unsupported, stop the submission
   route rather than relabel sensitivity as confirmation.
6. If data, governance, readers, power, or resources fail, mark the result
   blocked or inconclusive. Do not relax thresholds after inspection.
