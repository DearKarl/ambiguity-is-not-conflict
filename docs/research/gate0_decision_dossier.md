## Artifact Decision Detail

### Design invariants required under `G0-ARTIFACT A`

For every patient partition, the generator and validator must enforce:

1. each exact clear image occurs once with its compatible and once with its
   conflicting atomic assertion, so image-only and source provenance are
   label-paired;
2. each exact positive/negative atomic string, template, renderer, punctuation,
   length stratum, and process path has equal condition support across matched
   image-positive and image-negative source blocks;
3. variant generation happens inside the patient/source partition and emits a
   byte-level intervention ledger; and
4. any imbalance, duplicate leakage, hidden condition field, or process-path
   difference is instrument failure before model scoring.

For `MV-1` and `MT-1`, the manipulated modality is intentionally recoverable
as an information-loss arm. Their shortcut audit therefore excludes that
modality and requires exact non-target-modality identity plus matched process/
provenance checks against the within-source reference. A conflict-versus-
compatible probe cannot certify control-arm validity.

These invariants establish only the frozen empirical marginal balance. They do
not establish distributional equality for every unknown feature or prove
artifact absence. The four probes therefore remain useful falsification
instruments. Define orientation-safe recoverability as
`R_j = 0.50 + abs(BA_j - 0.50) = max(BA_j, 1 - BA_j)` on the frozen held-out
pipeline. A lower bound for `R_j` above `0.55` kills the instrument; an interval
crossing `0.55` is inconclusive; an upper bound strictly below `0.55` supports only
that exact probe/population claim. Raw balanced accuracy below `0.50` is not a
pass because flipping its labels recovers the condition.

### Powered equivalence option

If `G0-ARTIFACT B` is selected, the joint claim is the intersection that every
`R_j < 0.55`, against the union null that at least one `R_j >= 0.55`.
Equivalently, each raw `BA_j` must lie strictly inside `(0.45, 0.55)`. An
intersection--union test may test each component equivalence claim at the
stage's nominal alpha without alpha-Bonferroni correction for the conjunctive
global Type-I error; family power remains a separate requirement. Under a
centered Bernoulli-like approximation, a two-sided equivalence acceptance
region, and conservative beta allocation across four probes, illustrative
requirements are about 1,047 Month-3 and 1,757 confirmatory independent
patients before attrition, or about 1,232 and 2,068 screened at 15% loss.
The 1,047-patient figure is only a non-promotable developmental bounded-
recoverability screen: Month 3 cannot establish equivalence or supply
confirmatory artifact evidence. Only a later untouched, fully simulated and
powered confirmation could support the bounded four-probe statement.
Linearly scaling the current reader worksheet gives approximately 1,302
clinical hours through Month 3 and 2,657 hours for the confirmation row alone,
versus the revised proposed 500-hour stage and 1,350-hour cumulative ceilings. This
linear scaling is a planning assumption, not a workload fact. Requiring
two-sided Bonferroni-simultaneous
component intervals raises the corresponding crude figures to about 1,756 and
2,463. Balanced-accuracy structure, trained-probe uncertainty, patient
dependence, the non-smooth orientation, and model selection require simulation
before any figure is approved. Neither option fits the present construct
floors; the older one-sided 1,293/1,976 calculation is not orientation-safe and
cannot be the final design.
